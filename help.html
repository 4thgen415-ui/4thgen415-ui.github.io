Blaine is an assistant for real work that checks its own answers and tells you what it could stand behind. This page explains what those marks mean and how to get around the app.

Reading the marks

Under an answer you will see a short summary — something like "2 supported, 1 reviewed". Tap it to see which parts of the answer each mark refers to. The reply appears first; marks follow as soon as checking finishes. Every answer is checked; there is nothing to switch on.

Supported
The claim held up when checked. This is the strongest mark.
Reviewed
The claim was looked at and nothing about it stood out as shaky — but it was not established one way or the other. Reviewed is not the same as supported, and it is deliberately worded so the two never blur together.
Disputed
Something about the claim did not hold up. Treat it with suspicion.
Wrong
The claim was proven incorrect. Where Blaine can, it corrects the answer rather than just flagging it.

You will not see a confidence score anywhere in Blaine. Putting a number on how sure a claim is reads as precision that is not really there, and it makes people accept answers they would otherwise question. Marks describe what was established about a claim, and stop there.

Checking makes Blaine more careful, not infallible. For anything medical, legal, financial, or safety-critical, check independently before you act.

Getting around

  • Chat — ask a question, attach a file, or pick up an earlier conversation.
  • Tasks — longer pieces of work, and their history.
  • Rules — standing instructions Blaine follows in every conversation.
  • Documents, Photos, Videos — what Blaine has made with you, ready to open, download or reuse.
  • Vault — your keys and connections.
  • Settings — your account, appearance, data export, and the build you are on.

Your data

Taking it with you

Settings → Export my data downloads everything Blaine holds about you as a single file: your account details, conversations, what it remembers, your documents list, and your settings. It never contains your keys or payment details.

Deleting it

Settings → Delete Account permanently removes your account and everything in it, and cancels any active subscription. You will be asked to type DELETE to confirm. This cannot be undone — export first if you want a copy.

Billing

Blaine is a flat monthly subscription with a fair-use limit. You can cancel any time from Billing; your plan stays active until the end of the period you have already paid for.

Something wrong?

If something is broken or an answer looks badly off, tell us — bad answers are the thing we most want to hear about. Email support@getblaine.ai and include the version shown at the bottom of Settings, which tells us exactly which build you are on.
